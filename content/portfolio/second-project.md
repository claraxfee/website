## About

A digital humanities web project preserving colonial-era texts in Zapotec, an Indigenous Mexican language. As a Digital Humanities & Web Design Intern at Haverford College Digital Scholarship, I developed a new WordPress website for the Ticha Project to improve user experience and content management. I collaborated with history scholars, linguists, Indigenous community members, and digital humanities librarians throughout the design process, applying accessibility standards and UX principles at each stage.

## Timeline and scope

June – December 2025. Internship with Haverford College Digital Scholarship, supervised by Anna Lacy.

## Skills and technologies

WordPress, CSS, accessibility standards, UX design, digital humanities, cross-disciplinary collaboration
